FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Short answers, no long story.

Do I pay on the website?

Not at first. Mastro starts as connection + trust. Payments can come later when the flow is stable.

How do skilled workers receive requests?

Through simple channels like WhatsApp and calls. We’re designing for real-world behavior, not perfect app usage.

What areas do you cover?

Start with one city/area for the pilot. Expand once matching and reliability are working well.

Can I request urgent help?

Yes. Add urgency when you request help and we’ll prioritize nearby availability.
